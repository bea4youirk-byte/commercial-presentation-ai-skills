# Upgrade an existing presentation

Improve only what the user authorized. Preserve the source and work on a copy.

## Audit before changing

Identify the deck's audience, job, central argument, protected facts, brand constraints, source template, assets, and requested degree of change. Read [references/upgrade-levels.md](references/upgrade-levels.md) and select the lowest level that can achieve the goal.

Inventory every source slide and classify it as keep, rewrite, merge, split, reorder, redesign, or remove. Record the reason. Never delete technical limitations, legal language, scope boundaries, or evidence merely to make the deck cleaner.

## Improve the decision path

Fix the largest constraint first: audience mismatch, weak opening, unclear argument, unsupported claims, excessive density, broken sequence, poor hierarchy, inconsistent visual system, or weak call to action.

Use one narrative job and one primary claim per slide. Shorten before shrinking. Replace generic titles with meaningful conclusions. Keep authentic product and customer evidence; do not fabricate proof.

## Preserve template integrity

When a source PPTX exists, edit inherited masters, layouts, and slide elements rather than rebuilding from screenshots. Keep a deviation log for intentional structural or visual changes.

Render and inspect every final slide. Compare against the source for protected facts, required assets, data, notes, and legal content. Deliver the upgraded file plus a concise summary of material changes.
