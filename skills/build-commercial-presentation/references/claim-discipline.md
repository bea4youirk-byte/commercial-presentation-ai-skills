# Claim discipline

Create a claim register containing claim, source, access date, scope, conditions, and publication status.

Require evidence for prices, savings, ROI, payback, output, performance, capacity, autonomy, efficiency, delivery, installation time, availability, warranties, certifications, compatibility, customer counts, completed projects, testimonials, and superiority claims.

Label unsupported material as an assumption or verification task and keep it out of audience-facing copy. Preserve ranges, test conditions, exclusions, optionality, and dependencies. Do not turn “up to” into a guaranteed result.

For calculations, show the input assumptions and formula in notes or an appendix. Recalculate totals independently and reconcile them with the source spreadsheet. Never apply a markup twice or mix purchase cost with customer price.

Use primary manufacturer documents for specifications and authoritative sources for regulations. Attribute externally sourced non-trivial claims and assets in speaker notes when the platform supports notes.
