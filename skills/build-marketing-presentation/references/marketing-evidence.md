# Marketing evidence

Distinguish observation, inference, hypothesis, and verified fact.

Use customer language from interviews, searches, reviews, sales calls, support tickets, or supplied datasets. Do not invent quotes or personas. Use competitor research to identify sameness and gaps, not to copy wording or layout.

Require dated sources for market size, trends, rankings, traffic, keyword volume, benchmarks, adoption, pricing, and campaign performance. Explain the measurement basis and uncertainty.

For creative claims, verify that the promise matches actual product capability and operational delivery. Keep internal hypotheses out of audience-facing slides until validated.

Define the desired behavior and a primary metric before proposing channels or creative executions. Treat vanity metrics as supporting signals, not the business outcome.
