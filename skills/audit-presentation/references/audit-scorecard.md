# Presentation audit scorecard

Score applicable categories from 0 to 4 and explain the evidence. Do not average away a blocker.

- Audience and decision fit: the deck is built for the actual reader and desired action.
- Narrative: the sequence is cumulative and resolves the opening question or tension.
- Message clarity: each slide has one job, meaningful title, and readable hierarchy.
- Offer and conversion: scope, value, terms, objections, risk, and next step are clear when relevant.
- Evidence and claims: claims are sourced, conditions preserved, and calculations correct.
- Technical integrity: models, specifications, units, diagrams, tables, and charts are accurate.
- Visual system: typography, spacing, alignment, imagery, color, and density are coherent.
- Template fidelity: masters, layouts, protected elements, logos, and brand rules are preserved.
- Localization: terminology is consistent and target-language text fits without meaning loss.
- Accessibility: contrast, font size, reading order, alt text, and color dependence are acceptable.
- File integrity: no clipping, overlap, broken links, missing fonts, unresolved placeholders, corrupted media, or export defects.

Verdict:

- Send: no blocker or high-severity defect.
- Send after fixes: bounded high-severity issues with a clear repair path.
- Do not send: factual, legal, structural, localization, or file-integrity blocker remains.
